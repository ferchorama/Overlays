Hero market

the big card on the left of the board

In asset mode a market that names an asset in its asset column plots that asset's real 7-day price series — the same cached feed the Digital Assets board uses. Markets without an asset fall back to the synthetic walk, or show nothing.

The travelling glow is the Digital Assets board's animation: a short bright dash sweeps the line, rests, and repeats. It is a live element on air — switch it off here if the chart should stay still.

Headlines

never updated

events fetches headline questions that carry their own markets; markets fetches the sub-questions directly. The hub derives the upstream sort key from the entity you pick — leave the override blank unless the upstream rejects it. There is one Update per press and no refresh timer, so the board cannot change under you.
